One paste instead of ten

Your clipboard holds one thing at a time, so collecting six fragments means six trips back and forth. Incredibulk adds a session: copy as much as you like, then paste all of it at once.

Two shortcuts

These work while any application has focus.

Ctrl+Alt+C
Open a session. Keep copying with Ctrl+C as usual.
Ctrl+Alt+V
Paste everything you collected, as one block.

Anything you copy is captured, however you copied it: the keyboard, a right click, or an application with its own binding.

The stack is yours to edit

A list appears while a session runs, without taking focus from what you are doing. In it you can:

  • Untick a line to leave it out, without losing it.
  • Click a line to fix a typo before it goes anywhere.
  • Drag lines into the order you want.
  • Type a line that was never on the clipboard.
  • Name the session so you can find it later.
